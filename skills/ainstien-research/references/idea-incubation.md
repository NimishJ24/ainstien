# Idea Incubation

Use this guide when sources, discussions, confusions, patterns, or experiments spark possible research ideas.

## Purpose

Ideas should be captured before they are judged too harshly. Most early ideas are incomplete, but they preserve where the researcher's taste is developing.

The idea bank is not a claim sheet. It is a holding area for hunches, tensions, weird observations, possible experiments, and open questions that may later become claims.

## Capture Triggers

Add to the idea bank when:

- A paper leaves an unresolved gap.
- The user asks a question that could become a direction.
- Multiple papers share a limitation or assumption.
- A result contradicts the user's expectation.
- A method seems useful in a different setting.
- A failure mode appears repeatedly.
- The user says "maybe", "what if", "I wonder", or "this feels important".

## Idea Record

For each idea, capture:

- Idea or hunch
- Source that sparked it
- Why it may be interesting
- What would make it surprising to experts
- What would make it fruitful
- What evidence would be needed
- Feasibility notes
- Current status: raw, promising, merged, rejected, promoted-to-claim, testing

## Promotion Gate

Promote an idea to `claim-sheet.md` only when it can be stated as a precise, falsifiable claim.

Before promotion, ask:

- What exactly would be true if this idea works?
- Who would be surprised?
- What would change downstream?
- What would falsify it?
- What is the smallest useful experiment?

## Output

Update `idea-bank.md`, then recommend whether to read more, sharpen, test, merge, or reject.
