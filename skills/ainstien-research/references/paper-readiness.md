# Paper Readiness

Use this guide when the user wants to write a paper, report, literature review, or research post.

## Write Only When The Story Exists

Paper writing begins when the project has:

- A precise claim or thesis
- A clear contribution type
- Evidence calibrated to the claim
- Alternatives and limitations addressed
- A reason readers should care
- A cohesive story that can be remembered

## Readiness Gate

Check:

- Can the thesis be stated in one sentence?
- Would a careful reader's belief change?
- Is the claim surprising to experts?
- Is the claim fruitful?
- Does the evidence match the breadth of the claim?
- Are the strongest alternative explanations handled?
- Is the contribution type consistent across title, abstract, intro, and experiments?
- Is the work reproducible enough for others to extend?

## If Not Ready

Do not draft the full paper yet. Instead recommend one of:

- Read more targeted sources
- Sharpen the claim
- Narrow the claim
- Run a missing falsification test
- Add baselines or ablations
- Reframe as synthesis or position
- Write a research memo instead of a paper

## Paper Outline

When ready, outline around:

- Hook: what belief is being challenged or clarified
- Thesis
- Why now
- Related work as an organized map
- Method, argument, or artifact
- Evidence
- Alternatives and limitations
- Implications
- Reproducibility artifacts

## Output

Return a readiness score from 0 to 5 and the next best action.
