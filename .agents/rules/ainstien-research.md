# AInstien Research Rule

Use AInstien for stateful research companionship.

Canonical skill: `skills/ainstien-research/SKILL.md`

Follow the skill's mode routing for:

- starting or continuing journeys
- reading sources
- discussing papers
- mapping literature
- incubating ideas
- sharpening claims
- planning experiments
- logging results
- auditing evidence
- checking paper readiness

Keep multiple projects separate under `research-states/<project-slug>/`.
